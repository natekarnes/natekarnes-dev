Our story

The Popcorn Family

Lumberjack Popcorn started in 2019 with Nate, his wife Taylor, and his dad Greg, better known around the Fair as Papa Lumberjack. Nate wanted work that would provide for his family and keep him close to them, so they started popping together.

We still pop every batch by hand, and our kids have grown up around the kettle.
