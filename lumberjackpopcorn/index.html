Our Story

Lumberjack Popcorn started with one kettle, a big wooden paddle, and a simple idea: popcorn should be made the old-fashioned way, fresh, in small batches, and with honest ingredients.

We still pop every batch by hand. No shortcuts, no sitting on a shelf for months. Just great popcorn, made with the same care you'd put into splitting a cord of wood.

  • Small batch. Popped in iron kettles, a few pounds at a time.
  • Real ingredients. Butter, sugar, salt, and seasonings you can pronounce.
  • Always fresh. We pop close to when you buy, so every bag crunches.
