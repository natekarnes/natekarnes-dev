About Us

A family popcorn company from the Pacific Northwest.

Hand-popped kettle and gourmet popcorn, a big red trailer, and a lot of happy fair crowds.

Our Story

Lumberjack Popcorn Company is a family-owned popcorn business rooted in the Pacific Northwest. We pop every batch by hand, in small batches, then load up our big red trailer and bring it wherever people gather: fairs, night markets, farm stands, and company campuses.

Our home base is the Washington State Fair in Puyallup, where we serve kettle corn, caramel corn, and frozen lemonade topped with popcorn to huge crowds every season. Along the way we've popped for teams at Boeing, Amazon, MultiCare, the University of Puget Sound, and more.

And yes, Bigfoot is a regular customer.
